# #2084 current-dev source-only encrypted local owner-row snapshot

New dormant `ownerOfflineRowSnapshot.ts` supplies a read-only owner-scoped collector across eight **personal** RxDB collections (task/category/Diary/settings/Bucket List/Bucket–Task links/Activity/Activity–Task links). Unlike ordinary `exportData.ts`, its query does not apply `isDeleted:false` or drop dangling/orphan link rows. The collector validates owner IDs, original IDs, tombstone flags, duplicates, JSON serializability and bounded row/byte totals; scans every collection twice and rejects any observed change before reporting a complete capture. Auth/MFA gate and unlocked 32-byte root are mandatory inputs. Each full raw source record is authenticated+encrypted locally in purpose-separated `offline-snapshot` domain and bound to a stable root-HMAC opaque row ID, original collection/owner and random local revision. Negative tests cover cross-owner/root, ciphertext metadata tamper, changed source and duplicate/malformed rows. No plaintext or semantic source ID appears in returned serialized records.

**Important non-acceptance:** This is only a *current-origin source-only library*, not a user-facing saved file, old installed PWA exporter, atomic multi-tab database freeze or a complete lossless backup. `RxDocument.toJSON()` does not promise preservation of RxDB internal revision/sync checkpoints; messages/friendships/social/pending actions and media are handled separately and not covered here. Cross-collection ABA races and unverified controller/origin prevent claiming all historical unsent edits recovered. No remote write, backend/Preview/dev/main/Production mutation or normal synchronization encryption. Account cutover remains blocked until whole-client encrypted saved-file verification and idempotent restore complete.

## 2026-11-26 raw RxDocument revision preservation, exact limitation

The optional current-origin RxDB reader calls `RxDocument.toJSON(true)` instead of bare `toJSON()`, and refuses records whose local `_rev` or `_meta.lwt` were stripped. The encrypted rescue can now preserve the current local revision and write-timestamp provenance **of documents visible to normal RxDB queries**, including Mosaic application-level `isDeleted:true` rows. Official RxDB 17 documentation: https://rxdb.info/rx-document.html . This does **not** make the file a supported importable RxDB database, permit setting its previous `_rev` on newly inserted documents, or prove that unsynced replication checkpoints are recoverable. Crucially, RxDB's separate internal `_deleted:true` rows are excluded by ordinary `.find()` queries, even with the selector missing `isDeleted:false`; a separate reviewed raw-storage/export adapter is required to capture all internal tombstones and checkpoints. Fail closed rather than misrepresent revision-stripped data as a verified lossless cutover. Real historical installed PWA controller/origin and cross-tab state remain unverified.
