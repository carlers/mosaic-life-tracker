01 / THE IDEA

Not another productivity formula.
Just a place to begin.

Mosaic started as a way to bring the moving pieces of everyday life closer together. It is an independent software project, designed and developed iteratively with a focus on everyday usability.

It is still evolving. The public repository tracks what's implemented, what is changing, and what might come next.
